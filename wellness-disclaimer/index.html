Draft Wellness Disclaimer

This draft explains the limit of the educational material on this website. It is not an approved disclaimer and it is not medical advice.

Draft pending owner and legal approval. This page is not an approved policy. It does not create legal rights or obligations. Online form submission is paused, and this draft does not authorise the contact form to collect or send personal information.

Educational material

Articles, framework descriptions and reflective prompts are general educational and wellness material. They are not a diagnosis, a treatment plan, psychotherapy, medical care or a promise of a particular result.

The Human Operating System™, Life Decoding and the seven-stage human transformation framework are ways of organising reflection. They are not clinical tests, and they do not measure health, consciousness or personal worth.

No service is confirmed by a category

The contact form includes categories for programmes and retreats, personal guidance and wellness business mentorship. Those labels describe the kind of question a person might ask later. They do not mean that a clinical, coaching or mentorship service is currently offered, staffed or booked through this website. Online submission is temporarily unavailable, and the form does not send personal information.

FormSubmit is the intended third-party form delivery provider. It is not processing active submissions from this form. Experiences and retreats pages say when nothing is confirmed. This disclaimer does not add a schedule, a price or a facilitator credential.

Urgent help

The contact form is not an emergency or crisis service, and it cannot send a message. A person who needs urgent medical, mental health or safety help should contact local emergency or professional services. This draft does not name a helpline, because no helpline has been approved for this page.

  • A contact address for a concern about wellness content.

No credential, clinic, regulator or emergency number is added here.
