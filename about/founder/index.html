The founder

Sarvesh Mishra

Sarvesh Mishra is a media professional, content creator and entrepreneur whose work brings together storytelling, human behaviour, philosophical inquiry and the exploration of personal transformation.

Through CureSoulLife, he is working towards building an institution that connects Ancient Wisdom, Modern Science and lived human experience with knowledge, practices, learning and community.

The journey

A career in storytelling. A growing interest in the human story.

Journalism and media offer a way to observe people, institutions, decisions and the realities shaping everyday life.

Sarvesh's professional journey in media and reporting informs his interest in listening carefully, asking questions, examining perspectives and understanding the stories people tell about themselves and the world around them.

Over time, that interest has expanded beyond reporting what happens to exploring how people interpret their experiences, form beliefs, make decisions and seek meaning.

His work now connects media, entrepreneurship, storytelling and an evolving interest in human understanding and transformation.

The questions

What shapes the way we think, feel, choose and live?

Some questions do not have one simple answer. They require observation, reflection, different perspectives and a willingness to keep learning.

  1. Human Experience

    Why do people interpret similar experiences differently?

  2. Recurring Patterns

    What influences the habits, reactions and choices that repeat in our lives?

  3. Understanding and Change

    How can greater awareness become more conscious action in everyday life?

  4. Meaning and Responsibility

    How can people live with greater clarity, compassion and responsibility without reducing life to a single explanation?

These questions inform the ideas Sarvesh explores through Life Decoding, Inner Alchemy and the wider purpose of CureSoulLife. They are not a claim that he has a definitive answer to every question.

The methodology

Look beyond the event. Explore the pattern.

Life Decoding is a framework for examining personal experiences, interpretations and recurring patterns.

It encourages people to observe what is happening, question their assumptions, explore possible influences and consider more conscious choices.

It is not a prediction of destiny, a diagnosis or a claim that every life experience has one hidden cause.

For Sarvesh, Life Decoding represents an approach to asking better questions about the human experience.

Experience → Observation → Reflection → Possible Understanding

This is an illustrative sequence, not a validated clinical protocol or a guaranteed progression. The five dimensions are explained on the Life Decoding page.

From insight to lived experience

Understanding matters when it changes how we engage with life.

Inner Alchemy describes Sarvesh's developing philosophy of connecting insight, reflection, practice and lived experience.

It explores the relationship between what we understand, how we respond, what we practise and how our choices unfold in everyday life.

The emphasis is not on becoming a different person overnight. It is on creating room for greater awareness, more thoughtful choices, meaningful practice and continued learning.

Inner Alchemy is a philosophical approach, not a scientific credential or promise of a particular outcome.

The purpose

Build an institution that helps people explore human transformation responsibly.

CureSoulLife is being developed as an institution dedicated to human transformation.

Its broader approach brings together ancient wisdom, modern science, human experience, knowledge, research, practices and community.

The intention is to create a thoughtful environment where people can explore different perspectives, develop understanding and discover opportunities for meaningful learning and lived experience.

The institution is not intended to be defined by one personality or one explanation of life.

Its long-term credibility must come from the integrity of its work, the quality of its learning resources, the responsibility of its practices and the trust it builds over time.

The purpose is bigger than the person who begins it.

Working principles

Stay curious. Think carefully. Act responsibly.

These are working principles for this page. They use the same eight names as CureSoulLife's institutional values. They are not externally certified standards.

  1. Integrity

    Represent ideas, evidence, experience and limitations honestly.

  2. Compassion

    Approach human experiences with care rather than judgement.

  3. Curiosity

    Keep asking questions and remain open to learning.

  4. Evidence

    Distinguish personal reflection, traditional knowledge, philosophical perspectives and scientific evidence.

  5. Responsibility

    Recognise the consequences and limitations of ideas, advice and practices.

  6. Service

    Keep the purpose of human development and contribution in view.

  7. Collaboration

    Recognise that meaningful institutional work requires different perspectives, capabilities and contributions.

  8. Stewardship

    Build responsibly for the long term rather than optimising only for short-term attention.

Looking ahead

A world where human transformation is part of everyday learning.

The long-term vision behind CureSoulLife is to help make personal transformation, self-understanding and conscious living more accessible as parts of everyday life.

This involves building an institution where knowledge, research, practical experiences and community can develop together, with responsibility and intellectual honesty.

The ambition is long-term. Its credibility will depend on what is built, how it is practised, what can be demonstrated and how consistently the institution serves people.

Human transformation is a lifelong journey.

This is a direction for future development, not a description of a campus, headquarters or current operation.
