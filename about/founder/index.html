The founder

Sarvesh Mishra

Media, IP & Wellness Entrepreneur

Founder, CureSoulLife | Creator of Human Operating System™

Sarvesh Mishra is an Indian media entrepreneur, former television journalist and the founder of CureSoulLife, an emerging human transformation institution.

As founder, he contributes to the vision, conceptual direction and development of CureSoulLife.

The journey

From Understanding the World to Understanding Human Life

His professional journey began in journalism, where he spent years reporting on social issues, public affairs and major events that shaped contemporary India.

Through his work in television news, including News24, P7 News and Zee News, he developed a deep interest in human behaviour, decision-making, resilience and the forces that shape individual and collective lives.

Over time, this curiosity expanded beyond journalism into entrepreneurship, original media creation and the exploration of personal transformation.

CureSoulLife

The Vision Behind CureSoulLife

CureSoulLife grew from a fundamental question:

What if people could understand the patterns shaping their lives and make more conscious choices about how they live?

Sarvesh founded CureSoulLife with a vision to bring together ancient wisdom, modern understanding, lived human experience and practical approaches to personal development.

The institution seeks to make self-awareness, reflection and meaningful personal growth more accessible through learning, guided practices and structured experiences.

The purpose is bigger than the person who begins it.

A framework for reflection

The Human Operating System™

Sarvesh is the creator of the Human Operating System™, a conceptual framework exploring nine interconnected dimensions of human life:

  • Body
  • Mind
  • Emotion
  • Energy
  • Relationships
  • Purpose
  • Work & Wealth
  • Consciousness
  • Legacy

The framework encourages people to examine how these dimensions interact and influence everyday choices, habits, relationships and personal development.

It is designed as a framework for reflection and learning, not as a medical diagnostic system or a clinically validated psychological assessment.

Philosophy and approach

Look beyond the event. Practise what becomes clear.

Life Decoding

Life Decoding is a framework for examining personal experiences, interpretations and recurring patterns. It encourages observation, questioning and more conscious choices. It is not a prediction of destiny, a diagnosis, or a claim that every experience has one hidden cause.

Explore Life Decoding

Inner Alchemy

Inner Alchemy connects insight, reflection, practice and lived experience. It is a philosophical approach, not a scientific credential or a promise of a particular outcome.

Looking ahead

A Broader Vision

Through CureSoulLife, Sarvesh envisions an international learning ecosystem connecting knowledge, guided experiences, responsible facilitation and human transformation.

His long-term aspiration is to create an institution where individuals can explore meaningful change through awareness, understanding and consistent practice.

Human transformation is a lifelong journey.

This is a direction for future development, not a description of a campus, headquarters or current operation.

The founder

A Message from the Founder

I believe that understanding ourselves is one of the most important journeys we can undertake. When awareness is combined with honest reflection, conscious choices and consistent action, meaningful change becomes possible.

My vision for CureSoulLife is to create a space where people can explore this journey with curiosity, responsibility and a deeper understanding of life.

Sarvesh Mishra
