Draft Privacy Policy

This draft describes the paused enquiry form, direct email, and the Google tag already on the website. It is not an approved privacy policy.

Draft pending owner and legal approval. This page is not an approved policy. It does not create legal rights or obligations. The contact form can send an enquiry to curesoullife@gmail.com. This draft describes that process and is not a finished legal approval of it.

What this draft covers

This draft is about the public CureSoulLife website and the enquiry form on the contact page. The public name on the website is CureSoulLife. No legal entity name or postal address is stated, because none has been approved.

The form asks for full name, email address, country or region, enquiry category and message. Telephone is optional. Choosing Send enquiry delivers those details to curesoullife@gmail.com. The website does not keep its own copy. The owner confirmed on 24 November 2026 that email written directly to that mailbox is being received.

Details awaiting owner approval

Direct email receipt has been confirmed by the owner. These operational details have not been approved and are intentionally not stated as facts:

  • Legal entity name, registration and postal address.
  • A separate privacy-request address, other than curesoullife@gmail.com.
  • The lawful basis for receiving an enquiry.
  • How long an emailed enquiry would be kept, and who else would be allowed to read it.
  • Whether any person reading the inbox would be in another country.
  • How a person could request access, correction or deletion, and which law would apply.
  • Publication of the Google tag changes already approved for preparation: turn off automatic collection of email addresses, telephone numbers and postal addresses; turn off form interaction events, including form_start and form_submit; keep page-view measurement and email redaction. Those changes are not published.
  • Whether FormSubmit should remain the service that delivers a submitted enquiry.

No retention period is stated, because none has been approved.

What the website already does

A submitted enquiry is checked on the website, then sent through FormSubmit to curesoullife@gmail.com. The check covers the fields, a honeypot, the request size and a short rate limit. The website does not keep its own copy of the message. Direct email to the same mailbox remains available. That description is not a claim that a data-processing contract has been signed.

Every public page loads Google Tag Manager container GTM-WL45HBQ3. The published container, identified in the request as gtm=45He6bh1v9285393563, sends measurements to Google Analytics 4 ID G-3SF371FSJL. It records page views. It is also set to record clicks, scrolling, downloads, video, and form interaction, and to detect an email address, telephone number, or address typed into a page. On 24 November 2026 a synthetic test typed values into the live contact form. Google Analytics received a form_start event naming the form and the first field's id and type. The typed email, telephone, name, and message were not present in that observed request. The container itself was not changed. On 24 November 2026 the owner approved preparing a later version that turns off automatic collection of email addresses, telephone numbers and postal addresses, and turns off form interaction events, including form_start and form_submit, while page-view measurement and email redaction stay on. This workspace has no authorised access to the Google Tag Manager account, so that version was not created and the published container still has the automatic collection and form interaction settings. Publishing it still needs a separate approval. This draft does not approve the collection that is still published.

The website does not provide user accounts, a newsletter signup or a payment form. No Google Ads conversion ID is published in the container.

Children

No decision has been approved about an age limit. Do not use the form or the email to send sensitive information about a child.

Changes

This text can change during owner and legal review. The approved version, if one is adopted, should replace this draft and be clearly distinguishable from it. Until then, this page remains a draft.

Related drafts: Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Wellness Disclaimer. Each one is pending approval. The contact page can send an enquiry to curesoullife@gmail.com.
