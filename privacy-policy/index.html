Draft Privacy Policy

This draft describes the paused enquiry form and the temporary contact email. It is not an approved privacy policy.

Draft pending owner and legal approval. This page is not an approved policy. It does not create legal rights or obligations. Online form submission is paused, and this draft does not authorise the contact form to collect or send personal information.

What this draft covers

This draft is about the public CureSoulLife website and the enquiry form on the contact page. The public name on the website is CureSoulLife.

The form still shows fields for full name, email address, country or region, enquiry category and message. Telephone is optional. Online submission is temporarily unavailable, so the form does not send those details. The temporary contact email is curesoullife@gmail.com. Whether that mailbox is receiving mail has not been confirmed on this website. The website does not store a form message, because the form does not submit one.

Details awaiting owner approval

The temporary inbox has been named. These operational details have not been approved and are intentionally not stated as facts:

  • Legal entity name, registration and postal address.
  • A separate privacy-request address, other than the temporary enquiry inbox.
  • Confirmation that curesoullife@gmail.com is receiving mail.
  • The lawful basis for receiving an enquiry.
  • How long an emailed enquiry would be kept, and who else would be allowed to read it.
  • Whether any person reading the inbox would be in another country.
  • How a person could request access, correction or deletion, and which law would apply.
  • Whether the existing Google tag in the page head is retained, and what it records.
  • When online form submission may resume.

No retention period is stated, because none has been approved.

What the website already does

FormSubmit is the intended third-party form delivery provider. While online submission is paused, FormSubmit is not processing active submissions from this form. That statement is not a claim that a longer privacy arrangement has been signed.

Every public page already loads one Google tag in the document head. This draft does not approve that tag, describe its data, or add another analytics or advertising service.

The website does not provide user accounts, a newsletter signup or a payment form.

Children

No decision has been approved about an age limit. The form cannot send a message. The temporary email should not be used to send sensitive information about a child.

Changes

This text can change during owner and legal review. The approved version, if one is adopted, should replace this draft and be clearly distinguishable from it. Until then, this page remains a draft.

Related drafts: Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Wellness Disclaimer. Each one is pending approval. The contact page shows the temporary email. Its form does not send personal information.
