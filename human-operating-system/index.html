The Human Operating System™

Nine interconnected dimensions of human life.

A holistic framework for exploring the different dimensions of human life and how they relate to everyday experience, choices and development.

The Human Operating System™ is a conceptual framework. It is not a clinically validated diagnostic instrument. This page does not provide health scores, assessments, or guaranteed outcomes.

Body

Physical wellbeing

A dimension for reflecting on physical wellbeing in everyday life.

A dimension for reflecting on thoughts and attention in everyday life.

Legacy

A dimension for reflecting on contribution and stewardship in everyday life.
