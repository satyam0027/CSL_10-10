Global Network

A vision for responsible collaboration.

Explore the vision for responsible collaboration across people, knowledge and communities.

The Global Network is a developing institutional vision. Nothing on this page should be read as an active partnership, a physical centre, a laboratory, or a country office.

The vision is simple to state and careful to keep: people, knowledge and communities in responsible conversation. Locations are not proposed here as if they were open. Logos of other organisations are not shown.

Until collaboration is real and can be described accurately, this page stays a statement of intent rather than a directory.
