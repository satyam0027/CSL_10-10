Life Decoding

The methodology, named with care.

Life Decoding is the institution’s methodology. It is distinct from the Human Operating System™, which is the core system, and from Inner Alchemy, which is the transformation philosophy.

The methodology is a way of enquiring into a human life — through the dimensions of the Human Operating System™ and the movement described in Human Transformation. It is not a diagnostic test, and it does not produce a score.

A fuller method is not published yet.

Steps, worksheets and practitioner rules are intentionally absent. Publishing an invented method would misrepresent work that still belongs with the founder. When a public methodology is approved, it will replace this notice.

Until then, the honest description is the one above: Life Decoding is the methodology; the nine dimensions are the system it can work within; the seven stages describe a lifelong journey rather than a funnel.
