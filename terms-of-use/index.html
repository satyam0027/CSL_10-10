Draft Terms of Use

This draft sets out points for review before CureSoulLife publishes terms for use of the website. It is not an approved agreement.

Draft pending owner and legal approval. This page is not an approved policy. It does not create legal rights or obligations. Online form submission is paused, and this draft does not authorise the contact form to collect or send personal information.

Using the website

The public pages explain CureSoulLife’s ideas, including the Human Operating System™ and the human transformation framework. They are available to read. They do not create an account, a membership or a contract to provide a programme, retreat, guidance session or mentorship.

Online submission on the contact form is temporarily unavailable. The form does not email an enquiry. The temporary contact email is curesoullife@gmail.com. Whether that mailbox is receiving mail has not been confirmed on this website. Filling in the form, or writing to that address, does not create a booking, a client relationship or an obligation to reply.

FormSubmit is the intended third-party form delivery provider. It is not processing active submissions from this form.

Details awaiting owner approval

The following points are not decided in this draft:

  • The legal entity that would offer these terms, and its postal address.
  • The governing law and the place where a dispute would be heard.
  • Which reuse of text, names or images is allowed, and on what licence.
  • Any limitation of liability, warranty or indemnity.
  • A contact address for a legal notice.

This draft does not choose a jurisdiction or state that a person has accepted a contract by visiting the site.

Conduct

The website is not a place to send unlawful, harmful or deceptive material. An enquiry that arrives by email can still be declined.

Changes

An approved terms page, if adopted, should replace this draft. Until that approval, these words are a review document only.

Related drafts: Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Wellness Disclaimer. Each one is pending approval. The contact page shows the temporary email. Its form does not send personal information.
