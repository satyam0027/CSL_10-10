7-Day Online Guided Experience

Life Reset 7

A seven-day online guided experience across brain, body, relationships, wealth and energy.

Current standing

These three lines answer different questions. Closed registration does not show whether a program has been conducted before.

Program developed

The description on this page is published.

Previous delivery

The published conversation describes how Life Reset is arranged. A completed cohort is not recorded on this page.

Registration

Closed. No date, fee or payment is published.

Who it is for

Adults who want a guided online period to reflect on thought patterns, physical habits, relationships, money beliefs and energy.

Learning and experience objectives

The published account uses the five areas below as subjects for awareness and reflection, and for what a person may then consider in ordinary life.

Core experience areas

These five areas are named in CureSoulLife's published conversation. They describe what the program covers. They are not a confirmed day-by-day curriculum, and this page does not add exercise instructions.

Brain

Awareness of thought patterns and mindset. Reflection begins by noticing those patterns.

Body

Physical habits, energy sources and lifestyle. Reflection looks at how daily habits and energy fit together.

Relationships

Facts and stories in family and work ties. Reflection separates a fact from the story around it.

Wealth

Money beliefs and blocks. Reflection examines those beliefs.

Energy

Meditation, yoga and ancient wisdom practices, as named in the published account.

Format and delivery

Life Reset 7 is a 7-day online guided experience. A session length, meeting method and cohort date are not published.

It is separate from the one-day retreat, LIFE RESET — A Day to Pause, Reconnect & Restart, and from INNER BALANCE & TRANSFORMATION, the nine-day program for sustained reflection.

Facilitation

In the published conversation, Sarvesh Mishra describes Life Reset. He is Founder, CureSoulLife, and creator of the Human Operating System™. The facilitator of a future cohort is not named here.

Care and limits

This program is not a replacement for licensed mental health care, medical treatment, crisis support or other regulated professional services. Individual responses vary, and the program does not guarantee a result.

People experiencing severe psychological distress, suicidal thoughts or an immediate safety concern should seek qualified professional or emergency support.
