Have an idea that could contribute to human learning?

CureSoulLife is interested in exploring relationships with people and organisations whose knowledge, experience or purpose may complement its work.

Potential areas of discussion may include education, research, responsible knowledge communication, human development, community learning, technology and institutional collaboration.

Every proposed relationship requires appropriate discussion, evaluation and mutual clarity. Submitting an enquiry does not establish a partnership or imply institutional endorsement.

No formal partnership programme or dedicated collaboration team is claimed here.

A simple, responsible process.

  1. Your enquiry is received

    A success message would be shown only after a submission system confirms receipt. No system is connected, so that message is not shown.

  2. The enquiry is reviewed

    An authorised person would review the message and identify an appropriate next step. No reviewer is assigned on this page.

  3. Follow-up, where appropriate

    If a response were appropriate and sufficient contact details had been provided, someone might follow up. No reply time is promised, and an individual response is not guaranteed.

Your information should be handled with care.

Please share only the information necessary for us to understand and respond to your enquiry.

Contact details and message content should be handled according to CureSoulLife’s published privacy policy and applicable requirements.

We should collect only the information needed for the stated purpose, restrict access to authorised people, retain submissions only as long as reasonably necessary under the applicable retention policy and use appropriate safeguards.

Submitting a contact form should not automatically subscribe someone to a newsletter or marketing list.

The Privacy Policy is not published. This form does not store a message, send it to a recipient, or add anyone to a list.

Questions about contacting CureSoulLife

What can I contact CureSoulLife about?

You can contact us with general questions, media and press enquiries, collaboration ideas, questions about experiences and retreats, speaking invitations, feedback or other relevant messages.

How can I explore a collaboration with CureSoulLife?

Choose Institutional Collaboration and describe your organisation, idea, intended purpose and the people it may serve. Submitting an enquiry does not guarantee a partnership or formal relationship.

Can I contact CureSoulLife about retreats or experiences?

Yes. Select Experiences & Retreats. If a specific experience has a verified booking page on CureSoulLife.com, use that page for confirmed availability, registration and payment. No such booking page is published.

Does submitting the form guarantee a response?

No specific response time or individual response is guaranteed. If a response is appropriate, the team may follow up using the contact details provided. This form cannot currently submit a message.

How will my information be used?

Information should be used to understand and handle your enquiry in line with the published Privacy Policy and actual data-handling practices. The form must not automatically subscribe you to marketing communications. No policy is published, and this form does not keep what you type.

Can I use this form for emergencies or urgent clinical support?

No. This is an institutional contact channel, not an emergency or crisis service. It is not a substitute for urgent local emergency or professional support.
