Guided Practices

CureSoulLife is preparing an audio-first library for rest, grounding and a quieter kind of attention. A recording is added only after its audio file has been checked. The library is being developed.

Three areas of practice

These categories describe how the library is organised. The planned format is audio, without a picture of the founder. Individual recordings stay unpublished until each file has a verified address.

Rest & Sleep

Practices in this area are intended for settling the body before rest.

Calm & Grounding

Practices in this area are intended for slowing down and returning attention to the body and the breath.

Awareness & Clarity

Practices in this area are intended for noticing thought and returning to a steadier kind of attention.

Care and limits

These practices are educational wellness material. They are not medical treatment, a diagnosis, or a replacement for licensed mental health care, crisis support or other regulated professional services. A practice does not guarantee sleep, calm or a particular result.

People experiencing severe psychological distress, suicidal thoughts or an immediate safety concern should seek qualified professional or emergency support. Stop a practice if it feels unsafe.
